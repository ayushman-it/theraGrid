GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers, before you begin.

What happens after I send a request?

Our care team receives your contact and visit preferences and will get in touch to discuss availability and next steps.

Do I need to know which therapy I need?

No. Choose “Care consultation” or share the service you are considering; the team can help you explore options.

Is this a medical assessment?

No. This website helps you discover services and request contact. It does not diagnose, provide treatment advice, or replace a qualified professional.

Can I request an at-home visit?

You can select at-home preference. Availability depends on your location and the service; our team will confirm with you.

TheraGrid provides service discovery and appointment requests. Website information is general only and is not medical advice. A qualified healthcare professional should assess your individual needs. Privacy notice

When you send a request, the details in the form are stored in a private TheraGrid enquiry spreadsheet so the care team can respond. Your form data is not used for analytics. Optional marketing updates are only sent if you separately opt in. The team should retain enquiry data only as long as needed under its published retention policy. Do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, or medical history. For privacy questions, contact thecodemunk@gmail.com.
