THERAGRID / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how TheraGrid handles information you submit through care, appointment, onboarding, or provider partnership forms on this website.

Information you choose to share

Depending on the form, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, city, state, requested service or therapy, selected body area, preferred visit date and time, visit preference, privacy consent, and optional marketing consent. Provider enquiries may also include an organization name, provider type, and a short introduction.

Please do not include diagnoses, symptoms, medical history, or other sensitive health details in a form.

How we use it

We use submitted details to review your enquiry, respond to you, and coordinate the next step you requested. Optional updates are sent only when you separately opt in. The form receiver stores enquiry records in a private TheraGrid spreadsheet; its notification email contains the enquiry reference and type, rather than the form details.

Where information is processed

Form submissions are processed by the website form service and Google Apps Script, Sheets, and email services configured for TheraGrid. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D viewer library from Google-hosted services. Those providers may process technical connection information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Analytics, browser storage, and links

This website does not install an analytics library or use form details for user-level advertising analytics. It stores your language preference in your browser and uses session storage to manage the first-visit introduction. Links to other sites are governed by those sites’ own privacy practices.

Retention and security

Access to enquiry records should be limited to people who need them to follow up. Records should be reviewed and deleted when they are no longer needed for the enquiry, service operations, or applicable legal requirements. No fixed retention period is stated here. Internet transmission and storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and questions

You can leave optional marketing consent unchecked. To ask about a submitted enquiry or request correction or removal of its details, contact thecodemunk@gmail.com and include enough information for the team to locate the request. Do not send sensitive medical information by email.

Before publishing, TheraGrid should confirm that the described services, access controls, retention practice, contact details, and consent process match its actual operations.
